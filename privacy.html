ORBITA Marble

Privacy Policy · Last updated October 23, 2026

Summary

Version 1.0: the game has no online rankings, sign-in or purchases yet, so nothing leaves your device except the scores you choose to send to Apple's Game Center. The sections below describe the online features that later versions add.

ORBITA Marble collects only what the online rankings and Sign in with Apple need to work. We never collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location or advertising identifier, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. There are no analytics or advertising trackers in the game.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, stardust balance, unlocked orbs, missions and settings are saved on your device (in the browser's storage on the web). Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data, deletes them.

Online rankings

To place you on the rankings, the game creates a random player ID and a generated call sign (for example "Lunar Star 97"); you cannot type a free-text name. When you finish a run, the game sends that ID, your score and the run's moves, so our server can replay the run and check the score. Our host, Cloudflare, tells the server the country, state or region and continent of your internet connection; we store only those, never your IP address or precise location. Your call sign, score, world reached, country and region are shown publicly on the rankings.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional. It lets you keep the same player and ORBITA PLUS on the iPhone, the web and Android. We ask Apple for no name and no email. We store only a one-way hash of the anonymous identifier Apple gives our app, linked to your player ID, and a separate session for each device you sign in on.

Purchases

ORBITA PLUS and the extra-lives pack are sold only in the iPhone app and processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment information. If you are signed in, the app sends us the purchase's transaction number so our server can confirm the purchase with Apple and turn on PLUS for your account on your other devices.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, your scores are also sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn Game Center off in iOS Settings at any time.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings and choose Delete account (or Delete my ranking data if you never signed in). This immediately removes your player, scores, rankings, sessions and the link to your Apple ID from our server. A purchase stays with your Apple ID and can be restored. You can also remove ORBITA from your Apple ID's "Sign in with Apple" list in your Apple Account settings.

How long we keep data

Ranking data is kept until you delete it. Unfinished sign-in requests are erased within minutes.

Children

ORBITA Marble is suitable for all ages. Because players cannot type names or messages, the rankings never show anything a player wrote.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example, ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store and Google Play privacy details will be updated to match.
